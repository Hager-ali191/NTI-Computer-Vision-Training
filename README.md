# NTI Computer Vision Training Program
---

## Program Overview
- **Total Duration:** 120 Hours
- **Technical Track:** 90 Hours (Foundational Image Processing, Deep Learning, and Advanced Computer Vision)
- **Soft Skills & Professional Track:** 30 Hours (Freelancing, Portfolio Building, Technical Proposals, and Online Workplatforms)

---

## Part 1: Technical Track (90 Hours)

### 1. Image Processing Fundamentals
- **Digital Image Fundamentals:** Grasping pixel arrays, channels ($RGB$, Grayscale, HSV), and image quantization.
- **Image Enhancement & Filtering:** Spatial filtering, smoothing, sharpening, and noise reduction using Gaussian, median, and bilateral filters.
- **Color Spaces & Transformations:** Converting between RGB, HSV, Lab, and YCbCr color spaces for robust feature extraction.
- **Edge Detection & Morphological Operations:** Implementing Sobel, Canny edge detectors, dilation, erosion, opening, and closing for shape analysis.
- **Thresholding & Segmentation:** Global, adaptive, and Otsu's thresholding techniques alongside contour detection.

### 2. Deep Learning Foundations
- **Neural Networks Core:** Perceptrons, Multi-Layer Perceptrons (MLPs), forward pass, and backpropagation.
- **Activation & Loss Functions:** ReLU, Sigmoid, Softmax, Cross-Entropy Loss, and Mean Squared Error (MSE).
- **Optimization & Regularization:** Gradient Descent variants (SGD, Adam), learning rate scheduling, Dropout, and Batch Normalization to prevent overfitting.
- **Framework Proficiency:** Hands-on development using Python, PyTorch, and TensorFlow/Keras.

### 3. Advanced Computer Vision
- **Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs):** Convolution layers, pooling, stride, padding, and classic architectures (ResNet, VGG).
- **Object Detection:** Understanding bounding boxes, Intersection over Union ($\text{IoU} = \frac{\text{Area of Overlap}}{\text{Area of Union}}$), and modern YOLO architectures (e.g., YOLOv8/v9/v10) for real-time detection.
- **Image Segmentation:** Semantic and instance segmentation concepts, Mask R-CNN, and U-Net architectures.
- **Transfer Learning & Fine-Tuning:** Leveraging pre-trained models on custom datasets to maximize performance under limited data constraints.
- **Performance Evaluation Metrics:** Precision, Recall, $F_1$-Score, Mean Average Precision (mAP), and confusion matrices.

## Part 2: Professional & Soft Skills Track (30 Hours)

- **Freelancing Foundations:** Setting up and optimizing profiles on global platforms such as Upwork and regional platforms like Khamsat.
- **Client Communication & Negotiation:** Best practices for handling client briefs, setting milestones, and managing expectations.
- **Portfolio & Personal Branding:** Structuring technical portfolios on GitHub and LinkedIn to showcase real-world AI/CV projects.
- **Technical Proposal Writing:** Crafting compelling proposals that translate complex machine learning pipelines into business value for prospective clients.
